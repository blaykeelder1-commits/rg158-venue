## SECTION 2 — Negotiation Playbook: Your Strong Points & Incentive Levers

**Your job in negotiation: convert non-cash assets into perceived value to the comedian — so you pay 70% of their corporate quote and they say yes happily.**

### What you have that other 200-cap rooms DON'T

These are your moats. Lead every conversation by referencing at least 2 of them:

1. **Two on-site bars** (main + upstairs VIP) — most 200-cap rooms have one. This means upper-tier ticket VIP product is real, not theater.
2. **Concert-grade PA + programmable stage lighting** — most comedy clubs have a single mic and 1 spot. Comedians who shoot specials or content from your room get clean audio.
3. **J Rodgers BBQ on-site** — 30+ years, 4.3 stars. Most clubs have a $40 Domino's order in the green room. You have a private dinner experience worth $150 perceived value at $50 actual cost.
4. **Lockable upstairs VIP greenroom** — most clubs have a curtained-off broom closet. Yours is a real space.
5. **15 minutes from Mobile, 60-min radius covers 400K adults** — bigger draw radius than most comedy clubs in equivalent metro areas.
6. **Sister business cross-promo (J Rodgers BBQ)** — built-in 30-year customer list of Saraland regulars who are EXACTLY this audience.
7. **Festival comedy slot reservation right** — UNIQUE to RG 158. No other 200-cap club can offer "do well here, get a paid 30-min slot at our 10K-attendee festival in April 2027."
8. **Owner-operator buy-in (Blayke direct)** — agents prefer dealing with the owner. No middlemen, no "let me check with the GM."
9. **Already-built rg158ent.com with Square checkout** — agents check the site. Yours is professionally built. 80% of small venues fail this test.
10. **No radius-clause conflicts** — no other 200-cap comedy room within a 60-mile radius. Their last/next routing won't conflict with yours.

### Incentive levers — what to offer to lower the cash quote

| Lever | Cash cost to you | Perceived value to comedian | Best used when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hampton Saraland hotel partnership | $0–$95/night (corp rate; can negotiate to 0 with room-trade marketing) | $150–$200 | Always — table stakes |
| Ground transport (party bus, limo, Uber) | $50–$200/show | $200–$400 | Tier B and up |
| J Rodgers BBQ private dinner | $40–$80 cost | $150–$250 perceived | Always — your unique signature |
| **$60 buyout per principal** (industry standard 2026) | $60–$240/show | $60–$240 | Always; non-negotiable price floor for comic |
| Comp tickets for crew + family (5–10) | $0 (incremental seats) | $300–$700 | Tier B and up |
| **60-second vertical post-show clip for their socials** (rights kept by comic) | $0–$200 (your editor) | $1,500+ (one viral clip is worth tens of thousands) | EVERY comic — non-negotiable for you |
| **Festival comedy slot booking right** (April 2027) | $0 today | $5,000–$15,000 future booking value | Tier A and B — UNIQUE LEVER |
| Routing-detour kicker ($500 travel stipend) | $500 | $500 cash + closes the routing gap | Comics already in region (Birmingham, Atlanta, New Orleans) |
| Repeat-booking discount lock (10% off second booking if first clears 80%) | $500–$2,000 future cost | $500–$2,000 future value + relationship | Tier B touring acts you want to grow with |
| Merch rights — 100% to comic, no cut | $0 | $500–$3,000/show | Always — never take a merch split, it's the #1 deal-breaker for comics |

| **Documented hold + deposit on signing** | Cash flow timing | Trust signal — most clubs verbal-only | Always; signals you're real |

### Three deal-structure templates — pick by tier

**Template A — Flat guarantee (Tier C / first-time-with-you comics)**
> "$1,500 flat for one show, $2,500 flat for two shows. Includes hotel, ground, $60 buyout per principal, BBQ dinner, comp tickets for crew + 4 guests. Deposit 50% on signing, balance same night cash."

**Template B — Versus deal (Tier B workhorses — DEFAULT)**
> "$5,000 versus 70% of net door over $7,500 — whichever is greater. Net door = ticket revenue minus Square fees, comps, and holds. F&B stays 100% with venue. Settlement same night cash or NET-7 wire."

**Template C — Door split + low guarantee (Tier A anchors only)**
> "$10,000 + 80% of net door above $20,000. F&B stays 100% with venue. Settlement same night cash. Deposit 50% on signing, balance pre-show day."

### Counter-leverage scripts — when agents push back

| Agent says | Your reply |
|---|---|
| "What's your guarantee?" | "Open to flat, versus, or door split. Send me their standard quote and let's work backward from their draw on a 200-cap two-show." |
| "What's your draw history?" | "RG 158 has hosted [list 2-3 prior events]. The series launches July 2026 — this would be one of our debut headliners, so we're putting full marketing weight behind it. $800/show fixed Meta/IG ad spend + 25K social reach + J Rodgers BBQ cross-promo + Mobile country radio trade." |
| Quote is too high | "That's outside the budget for a [debut series / club-rate] booking. Can we look at a versus deal that pays them more on a sellout but gives us breakeven protection? Happy to show you the room economics." |
| Wants full guarantee at top of range | Counter: "We can do a guarantee at [-25% of their ask] plus 70% net door above breakeven — that's where the upside is." |
| Asks for radius clause > 50 mi | "Standard 50 mi / 60 days is fine — anything beyond that conflicts with our other bookings on the calendar." |
| Asks for all the door | Walk away. "That doesn't work for us — bar and F&B economics are how the venue stays in business." |
| Wants merch cut | Always offer 100% merch to comic. |
| Rider has private chef | Counter: "$60/principal buyout instead of catered greenroom." Don't negotiate the menu. |
| Settlement timing pushed to NET-30 | Counter: "Same night cash or NET-7. NET-30 doesn't work for our books." (Industry-acceptable lower bound.) |
| "We need flight + hotel for entourage of 5" | Pre-budget for principal + 1. Counter "$60 buyout x 5, hotel for principal + 1, ground for the group, additional rooms at our partner rate (comic covers cost)." |
| Asks for 2-show fee bump (+$1K) | Pre-budget +30% on Tier B quotes for 2-show; if you only want 1 show, say so up front. |
| Quote re-opens 90 days out (rare but happens) | Hold them to the original quote in writing or walk. This is bad-faith. |

### What to NEVER negotiate away

These are your hard lines. If an agent demands them, the booking dies:

- ❌ **Bar / F&B revenue** — 100% stays with venue. Always.
- ❌ **Radius clauses > 60 days / 50 miles** — kills your ability to book similar comics.
- ❌ **NET-30 settlement** — your cash flow can't carry that.
- ❌ **All-door / no-guarantee venue split** — kills bar economics.
- ❌ **Cancellation without notice clause** — must include 60-day-out cancel = full refund + venue marketing reimbursement; 30-day-out = 50% deposit forfeit; 14-day-out = 100% deposit forfeit.
- ❌ **No-recording-any-form clause** — you need the right to capture the 60-second vertical clip.
- ❌ **Any "100% advance payment" demand** — 50% deposit on signing is the industry max for any comic under $50K fee.

## SECTION 4 — Realistic Forecast Range (Veteran's Pass)

Three scenarios for the comedy series, by sellthrough:

| Scenario | Avg sellthrough | Net per ticketed night | 8-month total net | Funds festival? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative (likely months 1-3)** | 40% | $3,500/night | ~$170K–$220K | Partial — needs sponsorship to fill gap |
| **Realistic (likely months 4-8)** | 60% | $7,500/night | ~$280K–$350K | Yes — covers festival deposits + working capital |
| **Aspirational (sellouts)** | 85% | $12,500/night | ~$420K–$500K | Yes — overfunds festival, builds reserves |

**Plan to the conservative case. Bank the realistic case. Celebrate the aspirational.**

For the festival itself:

| Scenario | Tickets sold | Net |
|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | 6,500 (65% sellthrough) | $200K–$350K |
| **Realistic** | 8,500 (85%) | $400K–$550K |
| **Aspirational (sellout)** | 10,000 | $580K–$700K |

Plan to the conservative case here too. The economics still work because sponsorship + camping + F&B vendor cuts are independent of ticket sellthrough.
