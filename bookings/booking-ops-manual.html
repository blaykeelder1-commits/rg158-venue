2 · Negotiation Playbook

Convert non-cash assets into perceived value to the comedian — pay 70% of their corporate quote and have them say yes happily.

2.1 · What RG 158 has that other 200-cap rooms don't

Lead every conversation by referencing at least 2 of these moats:

  1. Two on-site bars (main + upstairs VIP) — most 200-cap rooms have one. Real upper-tier VIP product, not theater.
  2. Concert-grade PA + programmable stage lighting — comedians shooting specials from your room get clean audio.
  3. J Rodgers BBQ on-site — 30+ years, 4.3 stars. $150 perceived value at $50 actual cost.
  4. Lockable upstairs VIP greenroom — most clubs have a curtained-off broom closet.
  5. 15 minutes from Mobile, 60-min radius covers 400K adults — bigger draw radius than most equivalent-metro comedy rooms.
  6. Sister business cross-promo (J Rodgers BBQ) — built-in 30-year customer list.
  7. Festival comedy slot reservation right — UNIQUE to RG 158. No other 200-cap club can offer "do well here, get a paid 30-min slot at our 10K-attendee festival in April 2027."
  8. Owner-operator buy-in — agents prefer dealing directly with the owner.
  9. Already-built rg158ent.com with Square checkout — agents check the site. 80% of small venues fail this test.
  10. No radius-clause conflicts — no other 200-cap comedy room within 60 miles.

2.3 · Three deal-structure templates — pick by tier

Template A · Flat guarantee (Tier C / first-time comics)
"$1,500 flat for one show, $2,500 flat for two shows. Includes hotel, ground, $60 buyout per principal, BBQ dinner, comp tickets for crew + 4 guests. Deposit 50% on signing, balance same night cash."
Template B · Versus deal (Tier B workhorses — DEFAULT)
"$5,000 versus 70% of net door over $7,500 — whichever is greater. Net door = ticket revenue minus Square fees, comps, holds. F&B stays 100% with venue. Settlement same night cash or NET-7 wire."
Template C · Door split + low guarantee (Tier A anchors only)
"$10,000 + 80% of net door above $20,000. F&B stays 100% with venue. Settlement same night cash. Deposit 50% on signing, balance pre-show day."

2.5 · Hard lines — never negotiate these away

  • Bar / F&B revenue — 100% stays with venue. Always.
  • Radius clauses > 60 days / 50 miles — kills your ability to book similar comics.
  • NET-30 settlement — your cash flow can't carry that.
  • All-door / no-guarantee venue split — kills bar economics.
  • Cancellation without notice — must include 60-day-out = full refund + venue marketing reimbursement; 30-day-out = 50% deposit forfeit; 14-day-out = 100% deposit forfeit.
  • No-recording-any-form clause — you need the right to capture the 60-second vertical clip.
  • 100% advance payment demand — 50% deposit on signing is the industry max for any comic under $50K fee.

2.6 · Hospitality rider standards — what to expect

  • Buyout (cash for meals): $60/person 2026 standard. Pay flat to side-step menu disputes.
  • Hotel: Hilton/Marriott family preferred; one room per principal, queens for entourage.
  • Ground transport: dressing-room-to-venue and venue-to-hotel; airport pickup if flying.
  • Greenroom: water, light snacks, towels, mirror, lockable door, coffee/tea.
  • Tech: SM58 or comparable, single-spot stage light, water at stool — comedians' tech is dead simple.

3 · Pre-Flight Checklist

Lock these before sending email #1. Each unchecked box is a future loss event.

3.1 · Insurance

  • ☐ Confirm general-liability carrier covers stand-up comedy (some policies have entertainment exclusions)
  • ☐ Per-show event cancellation insurance — Event Helper, K&K, Hub International — $150–$250/show
  • ☐ Liquor-liability rider current
  • ☐ Certificate of Insurance template ready (agents request before quoting)

3.2 · Legal / contracts

  • ☐ Booking contract template via DocuSign — comedy-specific (Berger Goldstein, NathanTimmel.com, or industry standard)
  • ☐ Cancellation language: 60 / 30 / 14-day-out tiers
  • ☐ No-radius clauses > 60 days / 50 miles
  • ☐ Same-night settlement clause OR NET-7 wire

3.3 · Operations

  • ☐ 2-item-minimum POS workflow tested — Square Terminal prompt + server training + door wristbands
  • ☐ Box-office process locked — who scans tickets, tracks 2-item, handles will-call
  • ☐ Swing-comic on retainer — 2 local Tier C, $400 hold-fee + double-on-activation
  • ☐ Greenroom prep checklist — water, towels, snacks, mirror, lock, coffee/tea

3.4 · Venue / hospitality

  • ☐ Hampton Saraland (or partner) corporate rate signed in writing
  • ☐ Ground transport vendors confirmed (party bus + Uber Business + limo backup)
  • ☐ J Rodgers BBQ greenroom dinner SOP — same menu every time, $50 cost target
  • ☐ Hotel partner rate quoted in dollars, not negotiated per show

3.5 · Marketing / promotion

  • ☐ Email-capture banner live on rg158ent.com homepage
  • ☐ NON-show email capture pipeline — J Rodgers customer list import + festival waitlist + social contests
  • ☐ Meta Business Manager set up + first $200 audience-research test running
