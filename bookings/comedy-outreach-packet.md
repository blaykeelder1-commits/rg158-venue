## Reply-handling cheat sheet (when agents respond)

| If they say... | Respond with... |
|---|---|
| "What's your guarantee?" | "Open to flat or versus. Send me your standard quote and let's work backward from his draw." (Never name a number first.) |
| "What's your draw history?" | "RG 158 has hosted [past events]. The series launches July 2026 — this would be one of our anchor bookings. Promotional spend per show is $X, plus 25K+ social reach + email list." |
| Quote is too high | "That's outside the budget for a debut series anchor — would [comedian] consider a versus deal that pays them more on a sellout but gives us breakeven protection?" |
